Pricing — FAQ

Questions about our pricing.

The questions clients ask before they engage. If yours isn't here, just call — we'll answer it on the spot.

☎ Call 1300 952 255
Do you really charge fixed fees? +

Yes. Every Magistrates Court matter is quoted as a fixed fee in writing before any work begins. The only variables are disbursements (court filing fees, expert reports) — and those are quoted separately and capped where possible.

The fee doesn't move if the matter takes us longer than expected. That's the whole point.

What if my matter takes longer than expected? +

That's our problem, not yours. The fixed fee covers the matter to the agreed scope — including multiple mentions, sentence preparation and the appearance itself.

If the matter goes off-script — for example, what was a guilty plea becomes a contested hearing — we re-quote in writing before any further work, so you always know what you're committing to.

Can I pay in instalments? +

Yes. We offer payment plans on most matters where the work spans multiple months — typically a deposit on engagement, with the balance payable before the court appearance.

Speak to us about what works for your circumstances. We'd rather have you represented properly on a payment plan than under-represented because cash flow was tight.

What does the fee actually include? +

The fixed fee covers all conferences, correspondence, brief review, prosecutor negotiation, sentence preparation, the court appearance itself, and a post-court debrief.

Disbursements — third-party costs like court filing fees, traffic history extracts, psychological reports — are quoted separately and passed through at cost.

What if I want a second opinion on the price? +

Get one. Compare quotes on a like-for-like basis: scope, who appears in court, what disbursements are covered, what happens if the matter goes off-scope.

An hourly "estimate" of $300/hr × "8 to 12 hours" + disbursements is not the same as a flat $1,800. We're confident our pricing stacks up.

Is GST included in the price? +

Yes — every quoted fee includes GST. The number on your written quote is the final number you pay (other than separately-quoted disbursements).

Do you do legal aid? +

We don't accept legal aid grants directly. If you qualify for legal aid, we'd encourage you to use it. If you don't qualify but are still under financial pressure, talk to us about a payment plan — we work with people in difficult circumstances often.
