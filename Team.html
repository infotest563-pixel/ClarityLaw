What everyone on this team has in common

Four things we don't compromise on.

Every person on this page — lawyers, paralegals, office manager — operates on the same set of non-negotiables. It's how we've run 5,000+ matters without losing the personal touch.

01

Fixed fees, in writing, before we start

No hourly rates. No surprise bills. You get a written quote before we do anything — and the number on it is the number you pay.

02

One dedicated lawyer the whole way through

Belinda or one of our team takes your first call to get the details straight. From there, a single solicitor owns your matter — reviews the brief, walks into court with you, and gets the outcome.

03

Plain English — always

We translate every section number, every charge and every sentencing option into language your partner or parent can follow.

04

Available when you need us

Seven days, 7am–7pm on the phones. After-hours callbacks on request. A real person, not a voicemail, every time.
