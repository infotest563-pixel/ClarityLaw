Sunshine Coast assault — FAQ

The questions we get every Monday morning.

Plain-English answers to what people ask us most after being charged with assault on the Coast. If yours isn't here, ring us — we'll answer it on the spot.

Will I go to jail for an assault charge on the Sunshine Coast? +

Not automatically. Whether jail is realistic depends on which assault you're charged with, your history, the circumstances and the injuries involved.

Most first-time common assault and minor AOBH matters in Sunshine Coast Magistrates Court resolve with a fine, probation or a good-behaviour bond — frequently with no conviction recorded under section 12. Indictable matters such as GBH or serious assault committed up to Maroochydore District Court carry a higher risk, which is why early preparation matters.

Which court will I appear in if charged with assault in Maroochydore? +

Most matters list at Sunshine Coast Magistrates Court on Caloundra Road, Maroochydore. Matters arising further north (Noosa, Tewantin, Peregian, Coolum) are usually listed at Noosa Magistrates Court. Indictable assault matters such as GBH are committed to Maroochydore District Court.

We appear at all three on a regular weekly basis.

How much does an assault lawyer cost on the Sunshine Coast? +

We quote a fixed fee in writing before any work begins — no hourly rates, no surprises. Common assault pleas at Sunshine Coast Magistrates start in the low thousands. AOBH and District Court matters are quoted individually after we've reviewed the brief.

The legal fees page sets out the indicative ranges in detail. The first phone call is always free.

Can I get a no-conviction result for assault in Queensland? +

Yes — under section 12 of the Penalties and Sentences Act, the court can sentence you without recording a conviction. It is most often achievable for common assault and minor AOBH where there is limited or no prior history.

The court considers the nature of the offence, your character, and the impact of a conviction on employment, travel and personal circumstances. Well-prepared submissions, character references and rehabilitation material make a real difference.

What should I do if I've been charged with assault on the Sunshine Coast? +

Three things, in this order. First — say nothing further to police beyond your name, date of birth and address. Second — keep your bail paperwork, Notice to Appear or QP9 somewhere safe and don't post about the matter on social media. Third — call us before your first mention so we can request the brief of evidence and advise on plea before you commit to anything.

Most first appearances should be adjourned, not pleaded — even when the matter looks straightforward.

Do you handle serious assault and GBH matters? +

Yes. We run every category of assault matter Queensland law recognises, including serious assault, GBH, wounding, choking and assault police. Indictable matters from the Sunshine Coast are committed up to Maroochydore District Court, where Steven and the team appear regularly.
